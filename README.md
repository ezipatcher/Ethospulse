# 🌍 EthosPulse

### Measure Your Footprint. Master Your Impact.

EthosPulse is an AI-powered sustainability platform that helps individuals understand, track, predict, and reduce their carbon footprint through intelligent insights, gamification, and advanced environmental analytics.

Built to transform sustainability from a complex challenge into an engaging, data-driven experience, EthosPulse combines AI coaching, carbon forecasting, behavioral science, and community engagement in a single platform.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Climate change remains one of the most pressing challenges of our time. While individuals want to adopt sustainable lifestyles, they often lack:

* Awareness of their environmental impact
* Personalized guidance for improvement
* Motivation to maintain sustainable habits
* Tools to visualize long-term outcomes
* Community engagement and accountability

EthosPulse addresses these challenges through AI-powered sustainability intelligence.

---

## 💡 Solution

EthosPulse empowers users to:

* Calculate their carbon footprint in real-time
* Receive personalized sustainability recommendations
* Simulate future environmental outcomes
* Analyze utility bills and household appliances
* Track progress through gamification
* Compete in community sustainability challenges
* Visualize their environmental impact through interactive dashboards
